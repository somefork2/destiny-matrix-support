Common questions

How is the matrix calculated?
By the Natalia Ladini method, from the date of birth only: day, month and the digit sum of the year. Numbers above 22 are reduced by adding their digits. Time and place of birth are not needed.
Why do my numbers differ from another calculator?
Schools differ in how they reduce numbers above 22 and build the money and love line. We add the digits (23 → 5) and derive the money and love points from the crossroads of the channel.
How do I restore my subscription on a new phone?
Profiles → Restore purchases. The subscription is tied to your Apple ID.
How do I cancel?
Profiles → Manage subscription, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Access stays until the end of the paid period.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. We do not collect or share any data. See the privacy policy.
