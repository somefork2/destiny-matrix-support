Privacy Policy

Destiny Matrix (“the app”). Effective date: September 22, 2026.

What data we collect

None. The names and birth dates you enter are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers or shared with anyone. The matrix is calculated on the device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store and payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the app only knows whether a subscription is active.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking.

Deleting data

Delete a profile in the Profiles tab by swiping it left. Deleting the app erases all data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
